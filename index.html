GOVERNMENT FUNDING

Universities Fund Government Scholarship

Provider: Universities Fund, Kenya

Funding: Partial tuition support based on assessed financial need.

Deadline: Check official portal

Check the official portal for the current application status.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a Kenyan citizen.
  • Must have sat KCSE in 2022 or later.
  • Must have been placed in a public university by KUCCPS.
  • Must submit an application through the official HEF portal.

Documents to Prepare

  • Valid email address and mobile phone number.
  • KCPE and KCSE index numbers and examination years.
  • University admission letter.
  • National ID or birth certificate, as applicable.
  • Passport-size photograph.
  • Parent or guardian details and any other documents requested by the portal.

Important: Funding is not guaranteed. The award depends on the applicable eligibility rules and assessment of financial need.

STUDENT LOAN

HELB Undergraduate Student Loan

Provider: Higher Education Loans Board (HELB)

Funding: Financial support for eligible bachelor's degree students.

Deadline: Check official portal

Check the official portal for current application dates.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must meet the eligibility requirements of the relevant HELB loan programme.
  • For the undergraduate loan, applicants must be pursuing a bachelor's degree at a public or chartered private university within the East African Community.
  • Eligible students may include KUCCPS-placed, self-sponsored, parallel and Module II students.
  • Pre-university, certificate, diploma and postgraduate students are not eligible for this particular undergraduate loan.

Documents and Information to Prepare

  • Valid email address and telephone number.
  • KCPE and KCSE index numbers and examination years.
  • National ID or other identification required by the portal.
  • University admission letter.
  • Passport-size photograph.
  • Parent or guardian details and identification, where required.
  • Guarantor details, where required.
  • Valid bank or mobile payment details as specified by HELB.

Important: A HELB loan is repayable. Approval and the amount awarded depend on the applicable programme rules and assessment. Check the official terms before applying.

GOVERNMENT BURSARY

NGAAF Education Bursary

Provider: National Government Affirmative Action Fund (NGAAF)

Funding: Education assistance for eligible students from needy and vulnerable backgrounds.

Deadline: Check official portal

Contact your county NGAAF office for current application dates.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Must be a Kenyan citizen and resident of the county where the bursary is sought.
  • Must be a needy student attending an eligible educational institution.
  • Priority may be given to orphans and students living with disabilities.
  • Applicants must satisfy the current NGAAF bursary guidelines.
  • Support is subject to assessment and the availability of funds.

Documents to Prepare

  • A completed NGAAF bursary application form.
  • School or institution admission letter, for new applicants.
  • Current report card and fee structure, for continuing students.
  • Recommendation letter from the school or institution.
  • Recommendation letter from the area chief or community leader.
  • Death certificates or other relevant documents, where applicable for orphaned applicants.
  • Disclosure of other financial assistance received.

Important: These are general requirements from NGAAF's published guidelines. Confirm the current form, required attachments and application process with your county NGAAF office.
